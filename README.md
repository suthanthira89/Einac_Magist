# Eniac & Magist Market Expansion Analysis
## Project Overview

This project analyzes whether Eniac, a Spain-based premium Apple products and accessories marketplace, should partner with Magist, a Brazilian SaaS logistics and marketplace integration company, to support Eniac’s expansion into Brazil.

The analysis focuses on evaluating if Magist can meet Eniac’s operational and customer experience standards, especially regarding:

- The suitability of Magist for high-end tech products
- Delivery performance and customer satisfaction
- Market opportunities in Brazil
- Potential risks associated with the partnership

This project was completed as part of a Data Analytics learning journey and simulates a real-world business case where data is used to support strategic decision-making.

## Business Context

Eniac has built its reputation on:

- Premium Apple-compatible products
- Personalized customer support
- Fast and reliable deliveries
- High customer satisfaction

To accelerate international growth, Eniac is considering entering the Brazilian market through a 3-year partnership with Magist instead of building its own logistics and supply chain infrastructure from scratch.

However, there are important concerns:

1. Can Magist properly support premium tech products?
2. Are Magist’s delivery times fast enough to maintain Eniac’s customer experience standards?

The goal of this project is to answer these questions through exploratory data analysis.

## Objectives

The main objectives of this analysis are:

- Understand Magist’s business operations
- Analyze the Brazilian e-commerce market
- Evaluate the performance of tech-related products on Magist
- Investigate delivery speed and delays
- Assess customer satisfaction
- Provide business recommendations for Eniac’s expansion strategy
